The problem

Flattening FPGA macros into one-bit gates inflates simulation graphs and destroys useful structure.

The approach

GEM preserves DSP48E2, CARRY4 and SRLC32E primitives as native graph vertices, schedules the combined dependency graph with Kahn’s algorithm, and executes levelized waves on the GPU.

Verification

  • Aligned device ABI shared between formatter and CUDA kernel.
  • CPU oracle checks GPU results.
  • Benchmark protocol records measured and adverse cases.
  • A deep dependency chain reaches only 0.32×, documented rather than hidden.
