The problem

Implement the complete MAYO-1 signature flow as synthesizable RTL, while keeping the architecture parameterized for the remaining standardized parameter sets.

The architecture

The design coordinates SHAKE-256, AES-128 CTR, GF(16) arithmetic, banked memory, DMA, matrix transpose, Gaussian solving and encoding through a microcoded scheduler.

Parallel transpose

Tunable parallelism reduced a 64×72 transpose from 9,217 cycles at τ=1 to 1,153 cycles at τ=8. The trade-off is explicit: more parallel storage and routing for lower latency.

What is proven

  • Five self-checking architecture tests pass.
  • FIPS-derived known-answer checks cover AES and SHAKE.
  • MAYO-1, -2, -3 and -5 parameter models are present.
  • Cycle results are deterministic RTL simulation measurements.

What remains

The current scheduler measurements use one-cycle accelerator acknowledgements. Full transaction-level datapath completion, post-route timing, utilization and production side-channel hardening remain future work.
